Speed Wobble Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026

Speed Wobble is made by PhilltheBacon, an independent developer in Oklahoma, USA.

Information the game stores

Your progress (scores, coins, unlocked boards and gear, settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Google Play Games (leaderboards)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your best score and longest run are sent to Google Play Games Services to show on the leaderboards, along with your Play Games profile name and avatar. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your email address or other account details. Signing in is optional, and the game works without it.

No developer accounts

Speed Wobble has no accounts of its own and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or other personal information.

Advertising

Some versions of the game show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and usage information to show and measure ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Where required, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings > Privacy Settings in the game. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment information.

Children

Speed Wobble is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
